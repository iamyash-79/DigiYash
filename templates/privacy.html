Privacy Policy

01. Introduction

At SahuOne Technologies, we prioritize the security and privacy of our clients in the Solar, E-commerce, and Travel sectors. This policy outlines how we handle data collected through our performance engineering and automation services.

02. Data Collection

To provide high-velocity digital solutions, we may collect the following information:

  • Business Contact Information (Name, Email, Phone)
  • Technical Infrastructure Data for System Audits
  • Usage Analytics via Performance Monitoring Tools
  • Solar Lead Data (for automation clients only)

03. How We Use Data

Data is strictly used to enhance platform performance and automate business logic. We do not sell your business data to third-party brokers. Key uses include:

• Optimizing server-side rendering speeds.
• Training predictive analytics models for growth systems.
• Seamless CRM integration and lead flow automation.

04. Your Rights

Clients have the right to request a full export of their project data, request deletion of non-essential technical logs, and opt-out of performance tracking experiments.

05. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding data protection or technical security protocols, please contact our lead developer at legal@sahuone.com.
